How it works

Simple for boaters. Built for owners and pros.

For boaters

  1. Create your accountSign up in the Marine Concierge app and add your boat details.
  2. Browse & bookReserve a slip, book a service, request a quote or order provisions.
  3. Message & pay securelyChat with your owner or provider and pay in the app through Stripe.
  4. Review & earnLeave a review and earn rewards points toward Bronze, Silver and Gold tiers.

For slip owners & providers

  1. List your slip or serviceAdd photos, pricing, availability and your cancellation policy.
  2. Connect payoutsSet up Stripe to receive payments directly through the app.
  3. Accept bookings & quotesRespond to requests and message clients in one place.
  4. Get paid & growReceive payouts and build your reputation with reviews.
